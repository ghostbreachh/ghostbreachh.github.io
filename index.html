who we are

Ghostbreachh is an independent offensive-security collective founded and led by a team of Pakistani researchers. We help startups, SMEs, agencies and growing businesses improve their security posture through affordable vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, WordPress hardening and security consulting. Our mission is to make enterprise-level security accessible to organizations of every size — and to train the next generation of defenders while we do it.
